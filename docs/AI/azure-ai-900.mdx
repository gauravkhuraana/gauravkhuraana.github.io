- **AI-900 Certification** validates foundational AI knowledge and Azure AI services

- **Computer Vision** enables AI to understand and analyze images
- **NLP Services** allow AI to process and understand human language
- **Responsible AI** ensures ethical and fair AI system development
- **Azure AI Services** provide pre-built AI capabilities for various scenarios
